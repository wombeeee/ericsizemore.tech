About

I'm Eric. Husband to Makayla, dog dad to Cooper and Riley, lifelong Oklahoman. My faith in Jesus sits at the center of all of it.

By day I lead the Onboarding & Engagement team for CDW's education practice. I got there the long way: 13 years in K-12 ed tech, starting as a school IT director (networks, Chromebooks, the printer wars), then a few years on the road running 200+ workshops and admin bootcamps for school districts.

“Always testing ideas” is how I'm wired. Right now most of the testing is about AI at work, and what it changes for a team on a regular Tuesday. I've followed markets since I watched 2008 unfold as a college freshman. I read a lot of theology and WWII history. And I plan my falls around Sooners football and my winters around the Thunder.

I write to think. The writing here is me working things out in public, and I'd love to hear where you think I'm wrong.
